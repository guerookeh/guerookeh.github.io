### A Motivation for Quantifying Information

Information theory originated in 1948 with [Claude Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Shannon)'s paper [*The Mathematical Theory of Communication*](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf), which he published during his time at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs).
