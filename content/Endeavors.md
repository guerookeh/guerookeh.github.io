### What I aim to do
- Be able to explore a certain problem or topic thoroughly and detailedly.
- Showcase my work and notes with regards to a certain topic.
- Safekeep previous knowledge, thoughts, and ideas.
- Improve my intuition and edge in mathematics and programming.
- Maintain a haven for anyone that might find such writings useful to their work.
- Provide credit to sources for writing that I was inspired by or worked upon.
### How I aim to do so
- Surveys and explorations of topics through mathematical derivations or programming applications; whether the writings be small, medium, or large.
	- Such surveys and explorations will most likely require to delve into certain other topics; this is key to expanding knowledge in other territories. 
	- It is to my discretion to decide how far along I will decide to explore a certain topic. 
	- It is also up to my discretion to decide on the quality of my work: I aim for the best I can.
- Through such surveys and explorations, I form links between topics of interest that will strengthen my understanding of both topics through a dual or $n$-th point of view. 
- Solutions to problems of interest for practice to obtained knowledge. 
- It is to my discretion to decide what the focus of my writing will be, and the balance between the theoretical and practical approaches of my work.
